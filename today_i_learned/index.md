## TODAY I LEARNED ABOUT KEEPING IN TOUCH WITH OUR PAST

Having just returned from an erasmus in Barcelona, I could look back at what I learned. During my stay, I had the chance to explore different art media, from the most modern, to more traditional. One thing that stood out was analogue photography. Being able to try out something that was such a hard process in the past, yet we take it for granted today was a little eye opening. We don't always realise how good we have it today.

Film photograpyh taught me to value every single shot I take, to be patient and carefully prepare each and every photo. This process simply does not occur with digital photography. While I do not want to argue that the old process was in any way better, it is healthy to sometimes try out things the old fashioned way, to really appreciate how far we've come.
